Things worth knowing before you pay

Tyland uses some undocumented Apple interfaces. Reading what's playing system-wide, and drawing on the lock screen, have no public API on macOS — every app in this category is in the same position. Apple can change them in any update. When that happens Tyland switches the affected feature off rather than misbehaving, and can do so remotely within hours while a fix ships. It is the honest trade for features macOS does not otherwise allow.

Nothing leaves your Mac. No account, no telemetry, no analytics. Your calendar, your location and your audio are read on-device and never transmitted. The waveform reduces sound to four loudness numbers on the audio thread; it is never recorded.

The live waveform needs macOS 14.4. Everything else runs on macOS 13 and later. Lock screen widgets are labelled experimental in the app, because they are.
